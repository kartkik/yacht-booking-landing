The Azura 65 Fort Kochi Experience

The Azura 65 is a premier comfort-class motor yacht berthed year-round at Bolgatty Marina Yacht Club in Kochi. Meticulously engineered for both open ocean coastal waters and sheltered backwater cruising, it offers European luxury with authentic Kerala maritime hospitality.

Guests board within minutes of arrival, welcomed with chilled fresh tender coconut water and scented cold towels. The crew handles luggage and delivers an effortless safety briefing before pushing off past the historic Chinese fishing nets, Willingdon Island, and into the Arabian Sea sunset.
